THIS LETTER'S FOR YOu
Our love sleeps behind the carthenware jars in order to mate the moon with the sun
Many times when two people live in the same house for a long time, they have to confront the time when feelings come tumbling down making room for habit to sit and shoot the bull.
Its a normal situation.
But for the feeling to come back and prepare the soil to make things bright and new, the two (2) people must rebuild the freshness of their youth in their days of love.
This is why today I want to tell you I love you still andiwant to make you feel the radiance of all the love unwinding from my heart for you.
Today your spirit rode me all day long, it didn't give my soul breath to take a rest. My clock is so rocked by you it's as if we were still sitting side by side at the foot of Mont Hópital, where love for one reared its head in the other.
May our love keep rearing up, because rearing up so, it will find the wind that will carry it up to the space of our youthlul love, which age cannot exinguish.
Age stalks us, its true, but our kite should continue rising straight up in the sky of our experience where the pillar of our patience will make it roar in a space we've Created for us both, in the island garden of your womanly body.
